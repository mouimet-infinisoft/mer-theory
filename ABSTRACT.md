## Abstract

MER Theory is a **propositional framework** proposing that observer-relative scale perception may offer a unifying perspective across quantum mechanics and general relativity. The theory introduces postulates based on conjugate dynamics and explores whether deterministic universal laws can manifest as probabilistic observations at different scales.

This is explicitly a **work in progress**. The manuscript uses an explicit epistemic classification:

- **Postulates** — assumed axioms without proof
- **Propositions** — results derived from the postulates
- **Conjectures** — plausible but unproven or untested claims

As of v0.7.0, the scalar sector has been corrected to restore exact probability conservation in the quantum limit. However, key components remain provisional: the vector field equation and interaction stress-energy tensor require full derivation, the golden-ratio coupling postulate may need reframing, and experimental predictions currently take the form of qualitative functional forms rather than calibrated, falsifiable bounds.

The theory has not yet been experimentally validated. Readers should treat conjectures as hypotheses and starting points for discussion.
